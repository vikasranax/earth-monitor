# M34 — Architecture & Wonders

## Zero API, curated learning layer
40 sites spanning modern architectural marvels, the New 7 Wonders of the
World, ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) heritage sites, and other
UNESCO landmarks. Ties into the "learn about the Earth" angle — a
geography/history education layer alongside the geopolitical/data layers.

## Coordinates
Precise for major well-documented historical sites; three modern buildings
flagged inline as approximate (The Whale in Andenes, Al Bahar Towers, and
the Nusa Penida retreat area — smaller/newer sites with less precise public
coordinate data).

## Where it lives
- `src/lib/architecture-wonders.ts` — the dataset
- `/architecture` — full list page
- Map layer + dashboard summary — wired separately (see next update)
